Title:
Plastics Waste Prediction and Sustainability Insights Tool

Problem Statement:
Retail supply chains generate significant volumes of plastics packaging waste across suppliers, manufacturing, warehouses and stores, yet retailers have limited visibility into how much is produced at each stage. Packaging data held by suppliers is frequently incomplete or unavailable due to system and integration limitations, forcing teams to rely on manual estimates that are slow, inconsistent, and difficult to act upon. Without a reliable view of plastics usage, handling effort and associated cost, retailers cannot identify high-wastage areas, optimize packaging decisions or evidence of progress against sustainability commitments.

Data Requirements (Guidance):
Domain-specific datasets including purchase orders, product and packaging specifications, supplier manufacturing records, warehouse and store stock receipts, and unpackaging activity logs. Data should capture plastics type, weight, dimensions, quantities ordered and handling effort across supply chain locations. Synthetic data may be used to simulate supplier gaps and wastage scenarios. Compliance with retail data governance required.
(Feel free to generate synthetic data as well from the provided access)

Expected Output/ Demonstration of Solution:
A production-ready AI tool that predicts plastics waste volume, handling cost and effort at each stage of the retail supply chain, and surfaces actionable reduction opportunities to sustainability and supply chain teams. Includes comprehensive documentation and testing. Business benefits include reduced packaging waste, lower handling cost and stronger sustainability reporting.
